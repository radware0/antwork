# The daily loop

antwork is meant to be usable before you have a perfect plan. A stopwatch starts from Dashboard in one click. The Dashboard calendar is view-only; open Calendar when you want to inspect or edit a day.

## Lock in

**Start lock-in** begins an unlinked stopwatch. Timers also offers a countdown from 1 minute to 12 hours, entered as hours and minutes. You can pause and resume either mode. The timer uses persisted timestamps, so a refresh or sleeping computer does not reset its elapsed time. A countdown stops at its deadline and opens a session review when the app can settle it.

The review is where you may connect the session to a campaign, rate how it felt, or add a session note. None is required. The timer's unfinished time appears provisionally in today's display; career totals and Score include it only after it is saved.

## Record time another way

**Add work hours** on Calendar or Work Hours asks for a date, hours, and minutes. It saves a dated duration without inventing a start or end clock time. If exact times matter, choose **Use start and end times**. Corrections to an untouched endpoint retain its original seconds and milliseconds; an edited endpoint is saved to the selected minute.

## Reflect and review

The journal holds one private note per day. Saved text is readable on Dashboard and Calendar; **Edit** opens the note without altering it until Save changes succeeds. Calendar day detail shows the day's sessions and note. Above the journal, rate the whole day Good, Steady, or Rough independently of work hours. Ratings can be changed or cleared for today and past days. Switch the calendar between Day quality and Hours; it remembers your choice. Work Hours shows a 30-day chart and a match-style history.

## Keep your own copy

For a date with saved work, **View day card** in Calendar opens a daily achievement: saved hours and your whole-day rating. **Day-card backgrounds** saves up to two pictures or silent looping videos once; one selected choice applies to all day cards. Background changes use Save and Cancel like the timer; cards exclude unfinished timer time. The editor is also available from Calendar without saved work. Older per-date backgrounds are preserved until you explicitly confirm replacement, with a backup export offered first.

Profile's **Local backup** exports the complete record as JSON, including day-card pictures and videos. Import validates the file and asks before replacing local history. Backups include notes, profile images, and other personal content and are not encrypted. Store them accordingly. See [Data and privacy](/docs/privacy/).
